CAFM Asset + PPM Importer

Engineering Efficiency Ltd | v8.0.13
Open a New Entity page to create assets, or open any saved Asset to update it in place from the workbook.
Asset lookups use direct type-and-select. After a NEW Asset Save, v8.0.13 changes the Asset to ACTIVE and processes linked PPM rows. On a saved Asset, use Edit Existing Asset to refill populated workbook fields without creating a duplicate or changing existing PPMs. For large batches, enable multiple asset iterations and keep optional Notes/Spatial tabs off unless required. Contract, Instruction, Cost Centre and dates come from Excel. Every newly-created PPM is changed to ACTIVE after save.
