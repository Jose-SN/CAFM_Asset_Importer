CAFM Asset + PPM Importer

Engineering Efficiency Ltd | v8.0.31
Open a New Entity page to create assets, or open any saved Asset to update it in place from the workbook.
Load the workbook from the floating panel on the Asset list or New Entity form (ViewFASSETItem.aspx?id=-1). After Save, the editor closes, status changes to ACTIVE, then linked PPM rows are processed. On a saved Asset, use Edit Existing Asset to refill fields without creating a duplicate.
