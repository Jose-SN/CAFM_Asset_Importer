v8.0.9 - Close Current PPM Editor Tab

Strategy change:
- No child-window tracking is required to close a completed PPM editor.
- The PPM editor sends PPM_CLOSE_CURRENT_EDITOR_TAB to the background worker.
- Background closes sender.tab.id only after confirming it is not the registered parent and its URL is a PPM editor.
- Parent is focused and receives the next phase, then retries Create New when another PPM exists.
- Working asset save/status code is unchanged.
